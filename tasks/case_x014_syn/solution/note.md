Treatment Plan:
- Planned investigations:
  - Order 3-hour glucose tolerance test; patient to fast for 8 hours prior, preferably scheduled in the morning.
  - Draw TSH and free T4 today to assess thyroid dose adequacy.
  - Draw complete blood count (CBC) today to evaluate for anemia as a possible contributor to fatigue.
- Medication plans:
  - Continue prenatal vitamins with iron and folic acid daily.
  - Continue levothyroxine 75 micrograms daily; adjust dose if thyroid labs are abnormal.
  - Continue Tums for heartburn.
  - If Tums insufficient for heartburn, may try famotidine (Pepcid) 20 mg once or twice daily as needed.
  - For back pain: stretching exercises, maternity support belt, and warm compresses recommended.
  - Avoid ibuprofen and naproxen (NSAIDs) after 20 weeks of pregnancy.
  - Acetaminophen is acceptable for pain.
- Dietary/lifestyle plans:
  - Begin gestational diabetes dietary changes: spread carbohydrate intake throughout the day; pair carbs with protein and healthy fats.
  - Example breakfast: eggs with whole wheat toast and avocado instead of cereal and juice.
  - Snacks: nuts and cheese, or vegetables with hummus, instead of crackers or chips.
  - Lunch/dinner plate: half vegetables, quarter protein, quarter complex carbs (e.g., brown rice, quinoa, sweet potato).
  - Substitute white rice with brown rice or cauliflower rice; substitute sugary cereal with oatmeal with nuts and berries.
  - Avoid sugary drinks including soda and juice; limit artificially flavored waters; drink mostly water.
  - Replace orange juice with whole orange to obtain fiber.
  - Continue walking 3–4 times per week for about 20 minutes as tolerated.
- Patient education:
  - Begin daily kick counts in the evening while lying on left side; count time to feel 10 movements, which should be less than 2 hours; call clinic if decreased movement is noted.
  - Movements counted include kicks, rolls, or flutters.
- Referrals:
  - Referral to dietitian specializing in gestational diabetes; dietitian to call patient within approximately one week to schedule appointment.
  - Information provided for upcoming hospital breastfeeding class (approximately two weeks from visit).
- Follow-up:
  - Return in two weeks (at 30 weeks) to review all lab results and assess progress with dietary changes.
  - Patient instructed to call clinic with any questions or concerns before then, especially with any decrease in fetal movement.

Safety Plan:
- Patient instructed to call the clinic right away if she notices a decrease in fetal movement or does not feel adequate movement during kick counts.
